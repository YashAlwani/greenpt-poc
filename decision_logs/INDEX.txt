How to read these
-----------------
- A_ files are pre-build research notes (Analyzing stage): what was read,
  compared, and learned before any design decision was made.
- ADV_ files are pre-build advisory memos (Advising stage): concrete
  recommendations derived from the analysis, written before implementation.
- DL_ files are decision logs (Designing → Realizing → Managing): what
  was built, why, and what it unlocks.
- "FINDING" entries (DL-03, DL-04) document broken assumptions — moments
  where the data falsified an expectation. These are the highest-value
  entries in the log.
- The footer of every entry names the source files (with @ refs) and
  the LO stages covered.
